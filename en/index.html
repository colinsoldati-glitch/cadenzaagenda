Why Cadenza?

Time spent organising could be time spent playing.

Cadenza was born from a simple need: make day-to-day organisation easier for music ensembles. Fewer scattered messages, fewer back-and-forth conversations and a shared view of upcoming events.

From duos to chamber ensembles, music brings you together. Cadenza helps you stay organised.

iPhone / iPad

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?+

No. Create an ensemble or join with a code or link, then enter your name and instrument.

How do I invite other musicians?+

Use Invite in the app and share your ensemble’s link.

Can I use several devices?+

Yes. Use the profile transfer option in Ensemble. The personal link restores your profile: do not share it publicly.

How do I receive notifications?+

Enable notifications in the app and allow them on your device. On iPhone and iPad, install Cadenza on the home screen. Availability depends on your browser and system.

How is my data used?+

The app synchronises ensemble information through Firebase and uses local profile identifiers. The presentation site does not load Firebase and the demo does not send data. Contact us with any data-related questions.

How do I report an issue?+

Email contact@cadenzaapp.ch with a description of the problem and your device.
